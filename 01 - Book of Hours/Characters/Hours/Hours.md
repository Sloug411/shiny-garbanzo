#Unpolished 

Hours are a tricky thing to define, but they seem to be the gods of the world. They are numbered from 0 to 24, corresponding with the hours of the day. In general, Hours have five origins, either from light, flesh, blood, stone, or nowhere.

Gods from light are the hours that descended from the [[Glory]]. Such hours include the [[Sun-in-Splendour]], [[Door in the Eye]], and [[Forge of Days]]. Gods from light tend to only have one core [[Principles|Principle]].

Gods from flesh are the Hours that were once humans. This category includes the [[Colonel]] and [[Mother of Ants]]. Gods from flesh are to be separated from the speculative category of gods from steel where Hours are created by humans. The hours that are formerly humans are mostly the youngest hours, with only one Hour not from flesh younger than them ([[Wolf Divided]]).

Gods from blood are Hours that were created through a ritual or sacrifice. Two main examples include the [[Moth (Hour)|Moth]] and [[Red Grail]]. The gods from blood might be the oldest origin of Hours, as both The Moth and The Red Grail are said to be the oldest Hour.

Gods from stone are the Hours that were worshipped on the earth by the [[Carapace Cross]] before the [[Lithomachy]]. Most of these Hours are dead, but some like the [[Horned-Axe]] and (debatably) the [[Egg Unhatching]] are still alive in some form.

Gods from nowhere have the most murky origin out of all of the Hours. They are obviously from [[Nowhere]], but it's hard to know what that area actually is. It is best described as a place not in the physical world, but not in [[Mansus]].
