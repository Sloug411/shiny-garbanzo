**Description**: "A blandly positive work on the Endeavour Club, published by the Endeavour Club. The Club was founded in 1862 but claims to draw from much older traditions"

**Study**: "Club members are assigned names out of noble myth - 'Jaufre', 'Roland', and, unexpectedly, 'Breadbeddel'. They claim to be heirs to the Leashed Flame, which gave the island of Britain such appalling power in some [[Histories]]."

**Mastery**: "Their 'Endeavour' itself is a purifying fire which makes its fuel something more than human. This transformation, however, is no longer encouraged by the Club, and perhaps - the work is coy on this - no longer permitted."
