**Description**: "The second volume of a compilation of quasi-Rosicrucian allegories supposedly by [[Robert Fludd]]. This is a later English translation."

**Study**: "Fludd, or pseudo-Fludd, suggests alignments between the alchemical tradition of rubedo and the older [[Grail]] traditions, through the Pine-Knight's quest for the Cinnabar Cup."

**Mastery**: "'Seek the Cup in the birthing-bed,' the Pine-Knight is told. The Midwife at the birthing-bed, to his amazement, is the Mountain-Mother. In a fit of awe, he both gelds and flays himself. The [[Thunderskin|Vigilant Storm]] bursts joyfully from the remains."
