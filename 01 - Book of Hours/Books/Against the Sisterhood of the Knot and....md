![[againstsisterhood.png]]

**Description**: "'Against the Sisterhood of the Knot and the Foulness of their Depraved Customs'. Hieronymus Pseudo-Hypnerotomachus wrote this furious screed about the sinister influence of the Sisterhood on his own Church of the Unconquered Sun."

**Study**: "Hieronymus claims that the powers served by the Sisterhood of the Knot made an arrangement: the [[Thunderskin]] loved the [[Malachite|Yew]], and the [[Red Grail|Grail]] destroyed him, as restitution for the destruction of [[Horned-Axe|the Axe's]] loved ones. Hieronymus' writing becomes incoherent with rage at this point."

**Mastery**: "Hieronymus recovers his composure, eventually, to describe how the Thunderskin entered the [[Mansus]] through the [[Peacock Door]]. This, Hieronymus insists, is 'abomination upon abomination: but then that most abominable of Doors has always been an abomination.'"
