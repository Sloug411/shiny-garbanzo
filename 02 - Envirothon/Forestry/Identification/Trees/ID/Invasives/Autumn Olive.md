**Characteristics**: The Autumn Olive (AKA Elaegnus umbellata Thunber) is a [[Deciduous]] shrub that grows about 20 feet tall. The leaves are dark green, [[Leaf Arrangement|alternate]], and untoothed. The undersides of leaves are covered with silver-white scales. There are also small-yellow flowers along the twigs.

**FYI**: It can be controlled by hand-pulling them when the soil is moist to remove the root system. Burning does not work. Autumn Olive can also be controlled using herbicides such as glyphosate.
