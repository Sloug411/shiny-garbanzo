The **American Woodcock** (AKA Scolopax Minor and Timberdoodle) is a bogsucker and mud snipe.
They are most active at **dawn** and **dusk** looking for worms.
If the nest is disturbed they easily abandon the young.
The chicks are precocial (which is why abandonment is fine).
