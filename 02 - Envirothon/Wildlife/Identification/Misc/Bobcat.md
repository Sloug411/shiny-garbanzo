The **Bobcat** (AKA Lynx Rufus, bay lynx, wildcat, red lynx, and swamp tiger) is the state's only feline predator.
The bobbed tail gives the cat its name.
They are mainly nocturnal, but sometimes they go out in the day.
Females are very protective of their litter while males regularly eat their young.
