The **White-Tailed Deer** (AKA Odocoileus Virginianus) is commonly found throughout Pennsylvania and most of America, except for certain parts of the more western states.
They are named as such because their **tail is white** (duh).
The White-tailed Deer is **ruminant** (meaning they have split hooves) and have a four chambered  stomach.
They belong to the [[Cervidae]] family, which also includes [[Elk]], [[Moose]], [[Mule Deer]], and [[Caribou]].
White-tails have scent producing glands, which is how they communicate.
