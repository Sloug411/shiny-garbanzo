The **Red Squirrel** (AKA Tamiasciurus hudsonicus, chickaree, or pine squirrel) is a type of [[Squirrel]] that has red on its back but white on its front. It is about half the size of a [[Gray Squirrel]]. They store all of their food in large caches like hollow longs, and do not bury nuts.
